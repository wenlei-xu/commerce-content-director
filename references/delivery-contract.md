# Delivery contract

A final delivery is one playable, accepted final video linked to exactly one script.

Before a final-film record is created, verify target duration, portrait geometry, readable codec, product/subject continuity, dialogue or natural-sound review, and subtitle policy. Attach only the accepted assembled film, then fresh-read the record and attachment.

Raw segments, prompts and local reports remain in the run package. Storyboard candidates, including rejected boards, remain remotely available in the linked Feishu `分镜候选` records so review does not depend on one computer. They are not final-film records. Only the one selected complete board per Segment is copied to the script's `最终分镜图`.
