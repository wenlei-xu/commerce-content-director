# Delivery contract

A final delivery is one playable, accepted final video linked to exactly one script.

Before a final-film record is created, verify target duration, portrait geometry, readable codec, product/subject continuity, dialogue or natural-sound review, and subtitle policy. Attach only the accepted assembled film, then fresh-read the record and attachment.

Raw segments, prompts and local reports remain in the run package. Backend storyboard candidates, including rejected boards, remain traceable in linked Feishu `分镜候选` records or the configured internal candidate store. They are not final-film records and are not the human approval surface. Only the ordered attachments from one human-approved complete storyboard version are copied to the executable script/version record for final-video production.
